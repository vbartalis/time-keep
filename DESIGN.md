# Design System: time-keep

Every decision here was made in the operator's place (autopilot, 2026-10-20) and
rests on decision 9 (`docs/decisions/009-the-ui-follows-kimais-structure-and-skin.md`)
and the round 2 spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-design-direction-design.md`).
It describes the build on `feat/11-design-direction`, with what item 12
(`feat/12-entry-and-timer`) added: the entry form opened in place, the timer bar's
Start and start list, Restart, and "New entry"; and with what item 13 (`feat/13-calendar-views`) added:
the Calendar, its day, week and month views, its toolbar and date picker, and creating,
moving and resizing entries on its grid; and with what item 14
(`feat/14-timesheet-table`) added: Entries as a timesheet table grouped by day, its
Filter and chips (shared with the Calendar), the row menu, ticking rows, the bulk bar
and bulk edit, the phone's bottom sheets, and the `checkbox` stroke; and with what item 15a
(`feat/15a-setup-and-templates`) added: the phone's four-cell bottom bar and More sheet,
Today's templates and entries, the Templates screen, the template form and sheet, and
Setup. Where the build differs from the
direction contract (`.impeccable/surfaces/src-routes-app-layout-svelte.md`), the
build wins and the difference is noted. Light values are the frontmatter's plain keys;
dark values carry `-dark`. Dark follows the device setting through
`prefers-color-scheme`, so it works without JavaScript.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Kimai, Recognised at a Glance"**

time-keep is laid out and dressed like Kimai, the tool its one user already knows: a
near-black sidebar on the PC with a blue bar on the current screen, a white header
carrying the screen's title and the running timer, near-white ground under white
surfaces, neutral cool greys, Public Sans throughout. There is no themed concept over
the tool. The visual job is recognition and a timer that is visible and stoppable from
every screen.

Colour is held back for meaning. One blue marks what is running and the one primary
action; red marks only what is irregular or destructive; everything else is neutral
grey. The one strong colour in the system belongs to the projects: each project gets
one of eight colours and that colour follows its time everywhere it appears. Rank comes
from weight, size and thin rules, not from panels or shadow.

It is a working tool, dense on the PC and thumb-sized on the phone. It refuses the grey
SaaS dashboard of stat tiles.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Kimai's frame: a 232 px near-black sidebar, a 60 px white header, the screen on a near-white ground.
- One blue for running and primary; red only for irregular or destructive; neutral greys.
- Eight project colours, each with an edge, a tinted fill and a readable ink, assigned by creation order.
- Public Sans, tabular figures for every time, date and duration.
- 4 px corners (6 px on the entry dialog and sheet), 1 px rules, no shadows except on floating sheets, popovers, the dialog and a calendar block lifted in a drag.
- 150 ms ease-out colour changes on state only; the elapsed time ticks in place.

## Colors

Neutral cool greys on a near-white ground, one blue, one red, and a palette of eight
project colours as the only strong colour.

### Primary
- **Kimai Blue** (`primary`; dark `primary-dark`): the running timer (live dot, its
  ring, "Running" labels and running hours, exposed as `live`, and the live square in
  every Stop), the one primary button per form, the shell's one primary action (the PC
  header's New entry button and the phone's round "+"), the focus ring, the current bottom-bar item and the current row of the More sheet, ticked checkboxes (and
  a mixed one, a day's box with some of its rows ticked, filled blue with a white
  dash), the caret and text selection. A sheet's one primary action is blue: the
  filter sheet's "Show n entries" and the bulk form's "Apply to n entries". In the Calendar it marks the running block (a 2 px dashed foot
  at now and its "Running" time in semibold blue; a running entry's start in the
  month) and the pending new entry (a 2 px stroke over blue at 10 %, and the phone's
  round handles), which the create bar's primary New entry commits. On the near-black rail it lifts to **Rail Blue** (`sidebar-ring`)
  for the current item's bar, the wordmark's ring mark and focus on the rail; the
  phone strip's live dot uses it too. These are one blue at two lightnesses, not two
  accents.

### Secondary
- **Signal Red** (`destructive`; dark `destructive-dark`): exposed as `irregular` for
  warnings (overlaps, long entries) and as `destructive` for delete, revoke and
  restore and for refusal messages. Destructive buttons and error borders take it at
  60 %. In the Calendar it is an overlapping block's 2 px top edge, the outline round
  an overlapping month line, and the phone list's "Overlaps another entry". In the
  timesheet table it is an overlapping row's 2 px top edge (PC and phone), Delete in
  the row menu and the row sheet, and the bulk bar's Delete.

### Tertiary: the project palette
- **Eight project colours** (`project-1` .. `project-8`), each in three parts: the
  **edge** (the colour itself: dots, block edges, the phone row's bar), the **fill**
  (16 % of the edge in white; dark 26 % in the dark surface) and the **ink** (the edge
  mixed 45 % toward black; dark 75 % toward white), readable on that fill. In order:
  teal, amber, plum, green, indigo, rose, olive, umber. A project's colour is its
  position in creation order (ties by id), wrapping after eight
  (`src/lib/project-colour.ts`). It is derived, never stored. In this build the edge
  reaches the timer bar's project dot, the start list's template dots, the entry
  form's Fill from buttons and the project picker (its options and the chosen
  project inside the field); a template without a project takes a neutral dot (muted
  text at 40 %). The Calendar uses all three: a block is its project's fill with a 3 px edge at its
  left and its text in the ink; the month's lines carry 8 px edge dots, the phone
  month's days 6 px ones, and the phone day list's rows a 4 px edge bar. `chart-2` .. `chart-5` are projects 1–4; `chart-1` is
  the blue.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`ground`; dark `ground-dark`): the page under every screen.
- **Surface** (`surface`; dark `surface-dark`): the PC header, the bottom bar, sheets,
  fields, outline buttons, the undo strip and alerts.
- **Text** (`foreground`): body text and the section-head rule; in the Calendar,
  every mark that only points: the line at now and its tag, today's filled circle, the
  phone month's chosen-day ring, a drag's landing tag and the current view's
  underline (tags and circles carry `ground` text); the Filter's count badge
  (`ground` figures on a text-colour pill, "+2"), the phone day head's rule in the
  timesheet, and the PC bulk bar's frame (the text colour at 80 %). **Muted text**
  (`muted-foreground`): secondary lines, empty states, column heads.
- **Muted** (`muted`): the table head band. **Accent** (`accent`): hover fills, ticked
  rows (every ticked row in the timesheet fills with it, PC and phone, so a selection
  across days reads at a glance), a just-changed row while its undo is offered, an
  open Filter button, the current view in the Calendar's switch and the date picker's week
  in view. **Shade** (`shade`): the calendar's one shading, the hours before 08:00
  and from 20:00 and the days outside the month; `muted` in light; in dark, where
  `muted` on the surface does not read, a step lighter than `border`, so the hour rules
  and day dividers still read on it (muted text on it 4.7:1).
- **Border** (`border`): hairlines between lines, the header's bottom rule, the bottom
  bar's top rule. **Input** (`input`): field and outline-button strokes, the rule
  under a table head, filter chips' outline, the scrollbar.
- **Checkbox** (`checkbox`; dark `checkbox-dark`): the stroke of every unticked
  checkbox in the app (Entries, Copy-out, History, Users, Backup), one base rule. It
  holds 3:1 on the surface and on a shaded or ticked row (light 3.7:1 on the surface,
  3.3:1 on `accent`; dark 3.8:1 and 3.3:1), where `input` (1.6:1) did not. `input` is
  unchanged for fields and buttons; only checkboxes take `checkbox`.
- **The rail** (`sidebar` family): the PC sidebar, the phone top bar and (in its own
  tone) the phone timer strip. Light: `sidebar` is Kimai's #262626. Dark: the rail is
  darker than Kimai's, `sidebar-dark` #0f0f10, near-black on the #141516 dark ground,
  so it still reads as the darkest thing on the screen. `sidebar-primary` fills the
  current item, `sidebar-accent` is hover, `sidebar-border` divides.
- **Scrim** (black at 40 %, not a token): dims the page under the entry dialog, the
  entry sheet, the phone start sheet, and every bottom sheet and dialog (filter, row
  actions, bulk edit, More, a template's sheet, Setup's create).
- **Strip** (`sidebar-strip`; dark `sidebar-strip-dark`): the phone timer strip's own
  tone, a step off the top bar above it (darker in light, #1c1c1c under #262626; lighter in dark, #18191a
  under #0f0f10), so what is running reads as its own band rather than as the lower
  half of the title bar. The strip's "+N" list uses it too.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** Blue means running or the primary action, and focus (and, on the
phone, where you are: the current bottom-bar cell and More row). It is never decoration,
never a heading, never an icon tint beyond that current cell. A property of a row is not
running: a template's TIMER mark is muted, with a timer icon, never blue.

**The Red Means Wrong Rule.** Red appears only for something irregular or destructive.
A running timer is blue, never red.

**The Project Ink Rule.** A project is one colour, used identically wherever its time
appears. Today the timer dot, the start list, Fill from, the project picker, the
Calendar (block edge, fill and ink, month dots, the phone day list's bar) and Entries
(the table's 8 px dot, the project chip's dot, the phone row's 4 px bar) use it, and so do
Today's template rows and entries, the Templates table and list (an 8 px dot before the
name; none without a project) and Setup's project rows (the dot at 50 % when archived); the
other screens' tables and phone rows take it as item 15 rebuilds them. No other
element borrows a project colour.

**The Text-Colour Marks Rule.** In the Calendar, what only points is in the text
colour: the line at now and its tag, today's circle, the chosen day's ring, a drag's
landing tag. Blue and red already mean running and wrong; these marks never borrow
them. The Filter's count badge is such a mark: it counts, so it is a text-colour pill,
never blue.

## Typography

**Body Font:** Public Sans Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif),
self-hosted. One family for everything.

**Character:** Kimai's plain, sturdy grotesque. Rank comes from weight and the small
ramp below, not from a second face.

- **Figures** (`figures`: tabular numbers): every time, date and duration, so columns

### Named Rules
**The Figures Rule.** Every time, date and duration is set in `figures`. Columns never
move.

**The Five Steps Rule.** The ramp is 12, 14, 15, 17 and 21 px. No other size on working
screens.

**The Caps Are Heads Rule.** `caps` sets a column head, a section head (which is the
heading itself, an h2 on a rule), a group head in a list, a badge, a state label or a
template's mark.
Never a kicker above a heading.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the shell recognisably Kimai's: the near-black sidebar with a blue bar on the current item, the 60 px white header, the near-white ground.
- **Do** use #262626 for the rail in light and #0f0f10 in dark, with the phone strip a step off the top bar (`sidebar-strip`: darker in light, lighter in dark).
- **Do** keep one blue primary button per form; outline buttons for the rest.
- **Do** carry a project's colour everywhere its time appears, from the creation-order palette, and nowhere else.
- **Do** set every time, date and duration in `figures`.
- **Do** keep 44 px targets on the phone and the navigation inside thumb reach.
- **Do** open an entry's form in place: a dialog on the PC, a sheet on the phone, with Cancel and Save always on screen.
- **Do** keep every control at a phone screen's right edge out of the "+"'s column.
- **Do** keep motion to 150 ms ease-out colour changes on state, removed under reduced motion.
- **Do** mark now, today, the chosen day and a drag's landing in the text colour.
- **Do** draw a calendar block in its project's fill, edge and ink, with red only for an overlap and blue only for running or a new entry.
- **Do** give Customer › project room to read in full: a fixed 224 px column in the PC table, two lines on a phone row.
- **Do** fill every ticked row with `accent`, not only its box.
- **Do** show a phone choice over the page as a native modal bottom sheet over the scrim, closed by Escape, the scrim and Back, with its primary action pinned in view.
- **Do** stroke every checkbox with `checkbox` and keep `input` for fields and buttons.
- **Do** mark a template by what a tap does, TIMER or LOG, in muted `caps`, TIMER with its timer icon.
- **Do** let a phone row's meta line wrap between its parts, keeping a reference such as "T-1234" whole.
- **Do** return focus after an action: to Undo after a done change, to the head tick box or Select after a clear (when in view; else to the last ticked row, never scrolling the page).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use blue or red for decoration, headings or icons, beyond the current
  bottom-bar cell's blue icon; red is never the running state.
- **Don't** store a project's colour or let anything but a project use the project palette.
- **Don't** give a surface at rest a shadow; only floating sheets, popovers and dialogs, and a calendar block while lifted in a drag, cast one.
- **Don't** add text sizes beyond 12, 14, 15, 17 and 21 px on working screens.
- **Don't** set `caps` as a kicker or eyebrow above a heading.
- **Don't** build stat-tile dashboards or put a themed concept over the tool.
- **Don't** add entrance animations.
- **Don't** colour a template's TIMER mark blue; blue is for the timer that is running.
- **Don't** cut a project to one line on the phone or let a pinned foot scroll out of a sheet.
