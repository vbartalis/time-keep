# 9. The UI follows Kimai's structure and skin

- date: 2026-10-20
- status: active
- amends: —
- amended-by: —
- superseded-by: —

## Rule

The interface follows Kimai's structure and skin. On the PC a near-black sidebar and a
header holding the screen's title and the timer bar; on the phone a top bar, a timer
strip under it and a bottom bar with More. The ground is near-white in light and
near-black in dark, following the device setting with no in-app choice. One blue marks
a running timer and the one primary action on a screen; red marks only what is
irregular or destructive. Project colour is the one strong colour, derived from the
project's place in creation order over an eight-colour palette, never stored. Public
Sans is the one face, self-hosted, with tabular figures for times, dates and durations.

## Why

The operator judged round 1 "nothing like kimai" and its theming "horrendous". The
direction was chosen by the operator stand-in from three rendered options, with its
skin pulled back to Kimai's own: neutral grounds, a near-black sidebar, one blue. It
replaces a themed concept laid over a working tool with the structure the operator
asked for and the calendar apps' colour by project.
